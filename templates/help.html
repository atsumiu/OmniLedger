What information does it use?

  • Property information stored in OmniLedger.
  • Recorded income and expenses.
  • Property values and purchase prices.
  • Weekly rental amounts.
  • Calculated rental ROI.
  • Recorded transaction information.
API

Property Market Information

OmniLedger can use external property information when available.

PropRadar

OmniLedger connects to the PropRadar API to search for property market information.

What it can provide

  • Property listing information
  • Recently sold property records
  • Market-related property information

Limitations

  • Not every property will be found.
  • Off-market properties may not appear.
  • Some information requires a paid API plan.
  • Results depend on the information available through the API.
!

Important

OmniLedger AI provides analysis based on the information available to it. AI responses and external property information should be treated as general information and not as professional financial, legal or investment advice.
